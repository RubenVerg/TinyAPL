glyph: 'µ'
pattern: 'r←(nµ)y'
name: Power Mean
---

`n` is a numeric scalar. `r` is the `n`th power mean of the cells of `y`:

* if `n` is `¯∞`, `r` is the minimum of `y`: `r←⌊/y`
* if `n` is `∞`, `r` is the maximum of `y`: `r←⌈/y`
* if `n` is `0`, `r` is the geometric mean of `y`: `r←⦅≢√×/⦆y`
* otherwise, `r` is the `n`th root of the arithmetic mean of the `n`th power of `y`: `r←n√+/«÷»≢n↗y`
